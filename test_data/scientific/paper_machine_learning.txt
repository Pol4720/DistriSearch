ABSTRACT

Deep Learning Approaches for Natural Language Processing: A Comprehensive Survey

This paper presents a comprehensive survey of deep learning techniques applied to
natural language processing (NLP) tasks. We review the evolution from traditional
statistical methods to modern transformer-based architectures.

1. INTRODUCTION

Natural Language Processing has undergone a revolutionary transformation with the
advent of deep learning. Traditional approaches relied heavily on hand-crafted
features and linguistic rules, while modern methods learn representations directly
from data.

2. BACKGROUND

2.1 Word Embeddings
Word2Vec (Mikolov et al., 2013) introduced the concept of distributed word
representations, where words are mapped to dense vectors in a continuous space.

2.2 Recurrent Neural Networks
LSTMs and GRUs addressed the vanishing gradient problem in vanilla RNNs,
enabling the modeling of long-range dependencies in text.

2.3 Attention Mechanisms
The attention mechanism (Bahdanau et al., 2014) allowed models to focus on
relevant parts of the input sequence dynamically.

3. TRANSFORMER ARCHITECTURE

The Transformer (Vaswani et al., 2017) eliminated recurrence entirely, relying
solely on self-attention mechanisms. This architecture enabled unprecedented
parallelization and became the foundation for models like BERT and GPT.

4. CONCLUSIONS

Deep learning has fundamentally transformed NLP, achieving human-level performance
on many benchmarks. Future directions include multimodal learning and more
efficient architectures.
